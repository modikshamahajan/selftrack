SelfTrack is a modern, privacy-first, open-source personal growth companion designed to help you build lasting habits, track daily health metrics, reflect deeply through journaling, and optimize focus.

Unlike commercial tracker apps, SelfTrack operates offline-first, contains zero advertisements, zero third-party telemetry or trackers, and gives you 100% control over your personal data.

🌟 KEY FEATURES

1. Habit Tracking & Consistency
• Daily habit checklists with fractional consistency ring visualizers.
• Custom streak targets (3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 60, 90 days) with milestone rewards.
• Morning and Evening routine bundles with focused step-by-step runner guides.
• Customizable categories, colors, icons, and flexible scheduling.

2. Health, Hydration & Nutrition
• Real-time pedometer step tracking with customizable daily targets.
• Hydration visualizer with quick-tap water intake logging.
• Nutritional food logger with category breakdowns (Meals, Healthy, Drinks, Fast Food).
• Calorie tracking against customizable daily energy limits.

3. Menstrual Cycle & Symptom Tracking
• Cycle phase calculation (Follicular, Ovulation, Luteal, Menstrual).
• Daily symptom and mood recording with private local storage.
• Next period and fertile window forecasting.

4. Reflection & Journaling
• Daily guided prompts with 5-point emoji mood tracking.
• Photo attachments for capturing memorable moments.
• Local timeline history with quick date navigation.

5. Focus Timer & Ambient Soundscapes
• Built-in Pomodoro timer and deep breathing visualizer.
• 4 high-quality offline ambient soundscapes (Forest, Rain, Ocean Waves, Lo-Fi beats).
• Session statistics and completed focus minutes logging.

6. Gamification & Community
• Experience Points (XP) level progression and achievement badges.
• Daily mystery chests with streak milestone celebrations.
• Optional decentralized community feed to share milestones with friends.

7. Smart Notifications & Special Events
• Schedule birthdays, anniversaries, meetings, and occasions with countdown badges.
• Local on-device reminders without relying on proprietary cloud messaging.

🔒 PRIVACY GUARANTEE
• Offline-First: All core features function without an active internet connection.
• Zero Analytics: No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no user tracking.
• Data Portability: Export your entire dataset as JSON at any time.
• Complete Erasure: Permanently delete your account and all data with one tap.
